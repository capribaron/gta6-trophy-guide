Save discipline (no mission replay)

  • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done you cannot select it again.
  • The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one save at the start of each chapter and an untouched one from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest.
  • Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats are expected to switch off both trophies and saving.
  • Everything outside the story stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist — which only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, though work done earlier still counts.

How this guide was verified

  • All 55 names, descriptions and grades were checked on 19 November 2026 against the Exophase trophy list and PSNProfiles. The two agree on every name, description and grade, and on the PSN progress counts (for example 42 Shooting Range challenges, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida).
  • 28 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until earned (1 gold, 27 bronze); 27 are visible from the start. Hidden trophies are tagged in the tracker.
  • There are no online trophies: all 55 are single-player and offline.
  • Each guide is written in this entry's own words. Where a method comes from another guide, the guide is named and linked beside the trophy. Five trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — their guides say so plainly instead of inventing a method.
  • Missable flags come from PowerPyx and GTABase via the launch-week guides: only Positive Mental Attitude is flagged as possibly missable, and only because of one Chapter 1 meditation whose necessity is still unconfirmed.
  • Platinum estimate: about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). Long, not hard.

Using the tracker

  • Tick a trophy when you earn it — progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) and survives closing the page.
  • Export progress downloads your ticks as a JSON file; Import progress reads one back.
  • Hide story spoilers masks the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them. Hidden-on-PSN trophies are tagged separately.
  • Add ?demo=1 to the address to preview the tracker with 13 of 55 earned.
